For researchers & clinicians

Reuse, cite, and build on this work

Every page on this site carries a formatted citation in APA 7th, Vancouver and BibTeX, together with ScholarlyArticle structured data so that scholarly indexes, reference managers and citation trackers can pick the work up automatically. The whole catalogue is also available as a machine-readable file at publications/library.json and as an RSS feed of new work.

If you are a postgraduate student or researcher working in Prasuti Tantra, Stree Roga, fertility, or Ayurvedic management of women's disorders, these papers are a starting point rather than an end point — please build on them, and consider the clinical practice that sits behind them.
