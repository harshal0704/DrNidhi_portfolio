Abstract

Preconceptional care in Ayurveda emphasises a holistic approach to ensuring optimal health before conception. Nature has given the human body the great responsibility of pregnancy, and in the present era, where lifestyle-related issues and environmental factors affect fertility, couples seeking a "Supraja" — a healthy, physically, mentally and intellectually strong child — are drawn to Ayurvedic principles. A comprehensive review of Ayurvedic texts and contemporary studies was conducted, covering dietary recommendations, lifestyle modification and herbal interventions aimed at enhancing fertility, supplemented by practitioner survey and case-study insight. The findings indicate that Rutukal, Dinacharya, Rutucharya, Yogabhyasa, Panchakarma, Pranayama, Ahara-Vihara, Achar Rasayana, counselling, meditation, Garbhini Paricharya and targeted herbal interventions collectively contribute to enhanced fertility and improved overall health in both partners. The discussion underscores a sustainable path to conception that ensures parents are physically and mentally prepared before the journey ahead, and identifies the need for further research to quantify these benefits and integrate Ayurvedic principles with modern reproductive health practice.

Introduction:

Pre conceptional care in Ayurveda emphasizes a holistic approach to ensure optimal health and wellness before conception. Nature has given a great responsibility i.e., 'Pregnancy' to human body. Now a day Couples are willing that their child Should be physically, mentally & intellectually stronger than them. But in this present era where lifestyle related issues and environmental factors affect fertility. So, for 'Supraja' they should follow Ayurvedic Principles. Ayurveda, a holistic system of medicine, emphasizes balancing the body's energies (doshas) and enhancing fertility through lifestyle, diet, and herbal interventions.

Materials and Methods: A comprehensive review of Ayurvedic texts and contemporary studies was conducted to explore pre-conceptional care practices. Key aspects included dietary recommendations, lifestyle modifications, and herbal supplements aimed at enhancing fertility and promoting a healthy pregnancy. A survey of practitioners and case studies provided qualitative insights into the effectiveness of these interventions.

Results: Ayurvedic pre-conceptional care strategies were found to significantly improve reproductive health. Key findings indicated that Rutukal, Dinacharya, Rutucharya, Yogabhyas, Panchakarma, Pranayam, aahar-vihar, Achar rasayana, Counselling, meditation, garbhini paricharya and herbal interventions contributed to enhanced fertility and improved overall health in both partners.

Discussion: The findings underscore the importance of a holistic approach to pre-conceptional care in Ayurveda. By integrating traditional wisdom with contemporary understanding, ayurveda promotes a sustainable path to conception, ensuring both Parents are physically and mentally prepared for the journey ahed. This holistic framework not only supports fertility but also lays the foundation for a healthy pregnancy and child development.Further research is needed to quantify these benefits and integrate Ayurvedic principles with modern reproductive health practices, promoting a comprehensive model for pre-conceptional care.
