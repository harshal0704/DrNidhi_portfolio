Seminar Presentation

Prasava Vigyana — Ayurvedic Obstetrics

Management of normal labour: the classical four stages and their monitoring

Classical Ayurveda describes normal labour in remarkable detail. This seminar sets it against the modern partograph.

Abstract

Prasava Vigyana is the classical science of management of labour. This seminar presents the Ayurvedic framework for the conduct of normal labour, working through the four stages of prajasma with their characteristic signs, the assessment of the onset of labour and the progress of the uterine contractions, the ahara-vihara and positional management advised in each stage, the use of the classical formulations indicated for each stage, and the management of the third stage including the prevention of postpartum haemorrhage and the care of the newborn. The classical model is set alongside contemporary evidence-based practice and partograph-based monitoring.
