Conference Abstract

The Conceptual Approach of Preconceptional Care in Ayurveda

Structured abstract with Introduction, Materials & Methods, Results and Discussion

Ayurveda has a full pre-conception protocol — most modern fertility plans do not. This paper lays the classical framework out step by step.

Abstract

Preconceptional care in Ayurveda emphasises a holistic approach to ensuring optimal health before conception. Nature has given the human body the great responsibility of pregnancy, and in the present era, where lifestyle-related issues and environmental factors affect fertility, couples seeking a "Supraja" — a healthy, physically, mentally and intellectually strong child — are drawn to Ayurvedic principles. A comprehensive review of Ayurvedic texts and contemporary studies was conducted, covering dietary recommendations, lifestyle modification and herbal interventions aimed at enhancing fertility, supplemented by practitioner survey and case-study insight. The findings indicate that Rutukal, Dinacharya, Rutucharya, Yogabhyasa, Panchakarma, Pranayama, Ahara-Vihara, Achar Rasayana, counselling, meditation, Garbhini Paricharya and targeted herbal interventions collectively contribute to enhanced fertility and improved overall health in both partners. The discussion underscores a sustainable path to conception that ensures parents are physically and mentally prepared before the journey ahead, and identifies the need for further research to quantify these benefits and integrate Ayurvedic principles with modern reproductive health practice.
