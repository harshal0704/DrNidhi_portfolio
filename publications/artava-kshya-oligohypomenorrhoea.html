Seminar Presentation

Artava Kshaya (Oligohypomenorrhoea)

Aetiology, diagnosis and management of scanty menstruation

Scanty periods are the most under-reported complaint in women's health and frequently the first sign of a fertility problem.

Abstract

Artava Kshaya — scanty menstruation — is among the most under-reported complaints in Stree Roga, yet it is frequently the earliest signal of a fertility concern and is often dismissed by patients and clinicians alike. This seminar covers the classical definition and classification of Artava Kshaya, the doshic aetiology with particular reference to Vata and the role of Ama, the differential diagnosis from physiological variation and from other menstrual disorders including amenorrhoea, the investigation approach, and the therapeutic management combining ahara-vihara, aushadhi and specialised procedures such as Uttar Basti. The presentation also addresses the long-term consequences of untreated Artava Kshaya on reproductive health.
