Seminar Presentation

Sadarasa in Prasuti Tantra & Stree Roga

The classical principle of mental balance in the clinical management of women's disorders

Ayurveda separated the mind from the body as a treatment axis long before psychology existed. This seminar covers the classical method.

Abstract

Sadarasa — equipoise of mind — occupies a distinctive place in Ayurvedic theory, which holds that the mental component of disease is not an adjunct but a parallel axis of treatment. This seminar examines the concept of Sadarasa in relation to Prasuti Tantra and Stree Roga, exploring classical definitions of manas, the relationship between manas and sharira, the mechanisms by which psychological disturbance is described as a cause or an aggravating factor in disorders of menstruation, conception, pregnancy and the puerperium, and the classical therapeutic methods — including Sattvavajaya, Achara Rasayana and manas-antara treatment — used to restore balance. The presentation connects this framework to the modern clinical reality of stress-related menstrual and fertility complaints.
