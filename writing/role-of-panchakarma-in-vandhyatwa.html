Conference Abstract

The Role of Panchakarma in Vandhyatwa (Female Infertility): A Comprehensive Review

Conference presentation — Sushruta Ayurvedic Research Forum, Pune

Female infertility is rarely a one-herb problem. This review maps where classical Panchakarma fits into the modern fertility pathway.

Abstract

Vandhyatwa (female infertility) is one of the most common yet least discussed clinical presentations in Prasuti Tantra. This comprehensive review evaluates the role of Panchakarma in the management of female infertility, examining both Shodhana (purificatory) and Shamana (palliative) approaches as described in classical texts. The review covers the indications for Virechana, Vamana, Basti and Nasya in the context of reproductive disorders, together with the role of Ahara (diet), Vihara (lifestyle) and Dinacharya as the foundational substrate of any fertility protocol. The paper argues that Panchakarma works best not as an isolated procedure but as the preparatory and corrective stage that makes subsequent conception-oriented care effective, and it discusses the contemporary literature supporting these classical indications.
