Role of Uttarbasti in Yonivyapada

AADHYA — Uttarbasti Session, with a supporting seminar presentation

Vata is the only dosha with motility — and it drives most women's diseases. This abstract explains why retrograde oil instillation reaches where oral drugs cannot.

Abstract

Without Tridosha, no disease can form or manifest. Of the three, Kapha and Pitta are non-motile, while motility is attributed exclusively to Vata — and it therefore follows that vitiation of Kapha and Pitta most often requires an initiation from Vata Dosha. Basti has good therapeutic efficacy in normalising a vitiated Vata, which makes it a rational primary intervention in Yonivyapada, the umbrella term in Stri Roga covering almost all diseases of women. Uttarbasti carries both Shamana and Shodhana effects, and the drugs used in it are prepared in an oil base; depending on the condition, the formulation properties that act are Ushna, Tikshna, Vyavayi, sometimes Sheeta, Balya and Brimhana. The proposed mode of action is Srotoshuddhikara — the clearing of the srotas — together with stimulation of follicular cells and nourishment of the endometrium. Properly performed Uttarbasti produces desirable effects on several parameters of the female reproductive system.
