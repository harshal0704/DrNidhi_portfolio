Vomiting in Pregnancy: Nausea and Vomiting of Pregnancy in Ayurveda

Management of Garbhini Chardi and its classical formulations

Nearly three-quarters of pregnancies involve nausea. This seminar covers what Ayurveda offers — and what it deliberately withholds.

Abstract

Nausea and vomiting of pregnancy is one of the earliest and most universal symptoms of gestation, described in the classical texts as Garbhini Chardi. This seminar presents the Ayurvedic understanding of vomiting in pregnancy, the doshic basis of the condition, the classical diagnostic and therapeutic approach, the role of Ahara-Vihara and the specific formulations indicated in pregnancy with their indications, precautions and rationales, and the differentiation from the pathological vomiting of later pregnancy such as hyperemesis gravidarum. The presentation also addresses the limits of classical management and the point at which modern intervention is required, aiming to give the postgraduate audience a safe and practical synthesis.
