# Google Business Profile — the 15-minute setup that brings patients

This is the highest-return hour in the whole plan. It is also the one thing that **cannot be
automated**, because Google verifies that a real clinic exists at a real address.

Do this in one sitting. Have your phone, the clinic address and a few photos ready.

---

## Before you start: the shared-address rule

This matters, because getting it wrong can suspend the listing.

Google requires a listing to represent a real, customer-facing location with **signage and staff
present during the stated hours**. You practise inside **UTU Hospital**. Two possibilities:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **UTU Hospital already has a Google listing** (likely) | **Do not create a second listing at the same address.** Search Google Maps for the hospital. If it exists, use **"Claim this business"** or **"Suggest an edit"** to add your department and profile. A duplicate at the same address is the most common reason listings get suspended. |
| **You have your own consulting room with your own signage and entrance** | Create a new listing at that address. |

If neither applies yet, the honest move is to ask the hospital's administration whether the
department can be added to the hospital's listing. A correct hospital listing that mentions
"Department of Prasuti Tantra & Stree Roga — Dr. Nidhi Patel" is worth more than a suspended
personal one.

**Test first:** open Google Maps, search `Uka Tarsadia University Hospital Bardoli`, and see what
exists. Tell me what you find and I'll tell you which path applies.

## Step 1 — Create or claim

1. Go to **https://business.google.com/add**
2. Sign in with the Google account you will keep long-term (not a personal throwaway).
3. Search for the business name. If it appears, **claim it**. If not, **Add your business**.
4. Business name — use the real name, exactly as it appears on the signage:
   ```
   Dr. Nidhi Patel (M.S.) — Ayurvedic Women's Health
   ```
   Do **not** stuff keywords ("Best Ayurvedic PCOS Doctor Surat"). Google suspends for that, and
   it reads as advertising.

## Step 3 — Photos (10 minutes, do not skip)

Listings with photos get substantially more clicks. Upload at least 8:

| Photo | Notes |
|---|---|
| Profile / logo | A clean headshot or the site logo |
| Cover | Clinic exterior with the sign visible |
| Interior | Consultation room, waiting area |
| Team | You in clinical setting, professional attire |
| Building | Entrance, so patients can find it |
| Logo | Square version |

**Never upload patient photos, procedure photos, or before/after images.** Consent or not, it is
an ethics problem and a suspension risk.

---

## Step 4 — Verify

Google will offer phone, email, or **video verification**. Video is now common for clinics.

Prepare for video verification:
- Be at the clinic during the call.
- Show the exterior sign, the entrance, the consulting room, and your registration certificate.
- Have a utility bill or lease in the business name if asked.

Verification can take a few days. Do not edit the name or address while it is pending.

## Step 5 — The review engine

This is what actually moves you up the map.

1. In the GBP dashboard, open **Ask for reviews** and copy your short review link.
2. Send this once, after a good consultation:

```
Namaste [Name], thank you for coming in today. If the visit was helpful, would you mind leaving a
short Google review? It helps other women find the clinic. It takes about a minute: [REVIEW LINK]

Please don't include any medical details — just your experience. Thank you. — Dr. Nidhi Patel
```

3. Ask 5 patients in the first week, then 2–3 a week. **20 genuine reviews** is the threshold where
   the listing starts pulling its own weight.
4. Reply to every review within a week. For a negative one:

```
Thank you for taking the time to write. I'm sorry the experience did not meet your expectations.
Patient care is important to me and I would like to understand what happened. Please contact the
clinic on +91 70966 40893 so we can discuss it directly. — Dr. Nidhi Patel
```

**Never** buy reviews, offer discounts for reviews, write them yourself, or ask a patient to name
their condition in public.

## Common ways listings get suspended

- Keyword-stuffed business name
- A second listing at an address that already has one
- A virtual office or residential address with no signage
- Changing the name or address repeatedly
- Stock photos presented as the clinic
- Review incentives

If suspended, appeal at **https://support.google.com/business/troubleshooter/2690129** with photos
of the signage and your registration certificate.
