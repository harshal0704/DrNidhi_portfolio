## 6. FAQ — use on the site, Google posts and directories

**Do I need a referral to see you?**
No. You can book directly. If you are already seeing a gynaecologist or fertility specialist, bring
your reports — the treatments work better together.

**What happens at the first appointment?**
A detailed history: your cycle, digestion, sleep, stress, past treatment and reports. Then an
examination if needed, and a written plan. The first visit is unhurried — please book ahead.

**How long before I see a change?**
It depends on the concern. Cycle-related complaints are usually reviewed over three cycles.
Fertility care is planned over months, not weeks. You will be told what to expect at the start.

**Do you prescribe only herbal medicine?**
No. Treatment may combine diet, daily routine, herbal formulations and Panchakarma where
appropriate. If a condition needs modern investigation or referral, you will be referred.

**Can Ayurveda be taken along with my current medicines?**
Usually yes, but timing and interactions matter. Bring every medicine you take, including
supplements, to the first visit. Never stop a prescribed medicine on your own.

**Do you treat pregnant women?**
Yes, antenatal and postnatal care, alongside your obstetrician. Pregnancy care is shared, not
replaced.

**Do you offer online consultations?**
Yes, for follow-ups and for patients outside Surat. First visits are better in person where
possible.

**Is my information private?**
Yes. Your history and reports are confidential and are never published. Research and writing use
anonymised data only.

**What are your fees?**

## 12. Consistency problems to fix before you upload

These are real, and they cost you citation matches and local ranking.

| Issue | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **University name is inconsistent** | Citations say *Dr. N.T.R. University of Health Sciences*; the qualifications table says *YSR University of Health Sciences* | The institution was renamed. Pick **one** string and use it in `catalog.py`, the CSVs, the PDFs and every profile. `YSR University of Health Sciences (formerly NTR UHS)` is the accurate compromise. |
| **Phone formatting varies** | `+917096640893`, `+91 7096640893`, `+91 70966 40893` | Use **+91 70966 40893** everywhere; `+917096640893` only in `tel:` and `wa.me` links. |
| **Name variants** | Dr. Nidhi Patel / Dr. Nidhi Sanjay Patel / Dr. Nidhi | Fixed rule in section 1. |
| **Testimonials on the site** | `index.html` shows patient initials and city | Keep written consent on file for each, keep them anonymised, and never add clinical detail. |
| **No `sameAs` in the structured data** | `index.html` Person/Physician schema | After the profiles exist, add ORCID, Scholar, LinkedIn, ResearchGate, Academia and Instagram URLs to `sameAs`. This is what connects the profiles into one entity. |
