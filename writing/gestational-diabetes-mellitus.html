Gestational Diabetes Mellitus

Diagnosis, complications and the Ayurvedic management of madhumeha in pregnancy

The diagnostic threshold for gestational diabetes changed in 2025. This seminar covers the screening, the surveillance and the classical alternatives.

Abstract

Gestational diabetes mellitus is now the commonest medical complication of pregnancy and carries substantial maternal and fetal consequences, including macrosomia, hypertensive disorders and neonatal hypoglycaemia. This seminar covers the screening and diagnostic criteria, the glucose targets and monitoring required, the nutritional and lifestyle management of the condition, the pharmacological options, the intrapartum and postpartum management, and the long-term risk of progression to type 2 diabetes. Alongside the modern pathway, the presentation discusses the Ayurvedic understanding of madhumeha in pregnancy — the doshic basis, the classical ahara-vihara prescription and the role of herbo-mineral formulations — and evaluates where classical and contemporary management can be integrated responsibly.
