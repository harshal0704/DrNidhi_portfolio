Seminar Presentation

Complications of the Stages of Labour

Dystocia, haemorrhage and the management of abnormal progress in labour

Every stage of labour has a failure mode, and every failure mode has a clock. This seminar walks the three stages and their complications.

Abstract

This seminar covers the complications that may arise at each of the three stages of labour — the first, second and third — and their management in the Ayurvedic obstetric framework. It addresses abnormal progress and the recognition of obstructed and prolonged labour, the management of postpartum haemorrhage and the third-stage complications of retained placenta and uterine atony, and the indications for referral. Presenting the classical approach to prasava-vyapad, the talk also builds the practical decision-making sequence a postgraduate must follow when a labour deviates from the expected course.
