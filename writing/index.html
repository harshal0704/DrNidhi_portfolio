I have always believed that what I learn in the clinic should be written down and shared — with patients who want to understand their own bodies, and with students and researchers who are working on the same questions. So here is everything I have written: my M.S. (Ayurveda) dissertation on Uttar Basti for scanty periods, a published article on Pumsavana Karma, abstracts from conferences, and notes from my seminars. Read them in full, download them, and use them.

For students, doctors & researchers

Please use, cite and build on this

Every piece here carries a ready-made citation in APA, Vancouver and BibTeX, so you can reference it in a thesis or a paper without hunting for details. The full catalogue is also available as a machine-readable file at writing/library.json and as an RSS feed of new work.

If you are a postgraduate student or a colleague working in Prasuti Tantra, Stree Roga, fertility, or the Ayurvedic management of women's health, please treat these as a starting point rather than an end point. And if you are a patient reading this to understand your own situation better — that is exactly why it is here.
